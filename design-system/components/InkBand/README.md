# InkBand

A full-bleed dark section for brand story moments: either a solid ink band with gold pillars, or a forest panel with a brushed edge over a photo.

**Consumer provides:** `title`, `children` (one or two sentences of copy), optional `eyebrow`, `cta` (`{label, href}`, rendered gold), `pillars` (`{icon, label}` in gold rings), `image` (switches to the photo variant), `imageSide` (`right` default, `left`), `seed` (changes the brush edge).

- One band per page. Copy is calm and concrete: process, transparency, origin.
- Text on the band is `on-band`; accents are `gold`.
